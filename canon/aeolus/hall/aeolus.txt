He walks you down to the shore himself on the last morning, and
the winds are out on the sand, all of them, the way a kennel man
walks you past the dogs.

There are eight. He names them as you pass.

    ls aeolus/winds

You can read them. He does not mind. He made none of them and he
is proud of them anyway, the way a man is proud of a good dog:

    less aeolus/winds/boreas.wind

Then he gives you the bag. Oxhide, flayed from a nine-year-old
bull, and a silver cord to shut it with, and it is the finest
thing anybody has ever put in your hands.

Here is what it is for.

Every wind on this beach goes into that bag except one. The one
that stays out is Zephyros, the west wind, because west is where
Ithaca is, and a west wind at your back is a straight road home
and about ten days of it.

Everything else -- every wind that could push you anywhere other
than home -- goes in the bag and stays in the bag until you are
standing on your own beach.

    less scrolls/wildcards.txt

The bag is aeolus/bag. The winds are in aeolus/winds. All of
them except Zephyros go in, and there is a way of saying "all of
them except" that does not involve typing seven names.

Try the pattern with ls before you move anything. A pattern that
lists the wrong files will move the wrong files just as happily.

When it is done, look in the bag and look at the beach and make
sure the beach still has a west wind on it. Then sail.

    less aeolus/ithaca.txt
