The bronze wall again. The gate again. You walk up to the hall
with two men and you sit down at the edge of the table like a
beggar, which you have never done anywhere.

He is astonished. He is genuinely, openly astonished, and he
says: what are you doing here? You had the best wind in the
world and a road home and I gave you both.

And you tell him. Your crew, and your sleep, and the knot.

There is a silence in that hall that you will remember later, on
other islands, when things are worse. It is the silence of a man
revising his opinion of you in front of you.

Then he throws you out.

Not angrily. He says: get off my island. He says a man this
hated by the gods is not a man I can help, because helping him
would be helping against them, and I am not doing that for
anybody.

He does not give you a second bag. There was never going to be a
second bag. That is not how gifts work and you of all people
knew it.
