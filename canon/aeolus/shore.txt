An island that floats. Not anchored to anything, not
approaching, not receding -- it is simply where it is, and it is
walled all the way round in bronze, and the cliff under the wall
goes down sheer into the water.

There is a gate. It opens before you knock.

The man inside is called Aeolus and Zeus has given him the
winds. All of them. He keeps them the way you would keep dogs.

He takes you in without asking anything first, which is what the
rule about strangers says he should do and what nobody has done
since Troy. There is a hall, and a table in it, and a month of
your life is about to go past in that room:

    less aeolus/hall/feast.txt

You leave this island twice. The second time is the one that
matters, and it happens here:

    aeolus/refused.txt

Read that when you get back, and not before.
