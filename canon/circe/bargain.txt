She is glad to see you. They always are.

Silver chair, gold cup. Barley, honey, wine, something at the
bottom of it. You drink all of it. You hand the cup back.
Nothing happens at all.

She touches you with the rod and says: go and lie down with the
rest of them.

You are still standing there.

Then the sword is out. You go at her the way Hermes said, and
she is on the floor with her arms round your knees before you
have taken two steps. She is saying: who are you, what are you,
no man has ever drunk that and stayed on his feet. Then --

Are you Odysseus?

She was told. Years ago, by Hermes, on that same path: that a
man would come off the sea one day who could not be turned, that
he would be sailing home from Troy, that his name would be
Odysseus. She has been half expecting you since before the war
ended.

Then she invites you to her bed, exactly as you were told she
would.

She is a goddess. You are standing in her house. If you put that
sword down and go upstairs, she can turn you into anything she
likes on the way. The only thing in the world that stops a god
doing a thing is a god having sworn not to.

Make her swear it. Out loud, by the blessed ones, the great oath
-- that she will not try one more thing on you, that she will
not take your strength or your manhood or your name while you
are unarmed in her house.

Then write it down. A thing you can produce later is worth more
than a thing you remember:

    less scrolls/echo.txt

Put her oath in circe/oath.txt, in her words or in your own.

She swears it. She does not hesitate. She does not qualify it.
Afterwards she is different with you -- easier, almost relieved,
like someone who has been holding a door shut for a long time.

Then she brings the men back.

Another drug, rubbed on each of them. The bristles go. They
stand up as men, younger than they were. Taller. Better-looking
than any of them has ever been. They know you. They take hold of
you, every one of them crying, and the whole house is full of
the noise of it. She stands at the side and watches. She says
nothing.

Take them home. Out of the pen, back on the roster where they
belong:

    mv circe/house/sty/*.txt ship/crew

Then go down to the beach and fetch the rest. Eurylochus comes
without wanting to.
