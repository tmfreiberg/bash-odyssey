She lets you go. She swore an oath and she has kept every part
of it for a year. She does not argue for one minute when you
ask.

Then she tells you the price, which she has known all along and
has been waiting to say.

You cannot sail to Ithaca from here. Not yet. There is somewhere
you have to go first, and she says it plainly, standing on the
sand at dawn with the ship loaded:

You have to go down to the house of the dead and find the ghost
of a blind prophet from Thebes called Tiresias. He is the only
one down there whose mind is still whole. You ask him the way
home.

Nobody has done this. She is not aware of anyone who has done
this. She tells you how: the shore of the Ocean, a poplar grove,
a trench a forearm square, what to pour into it and in what
order. She tells you the dead will come up out of the dark in
numbers when they smell it, and that you are to keep every one
of them off with your sword until Tiresias has drunk.

Every one. Including the ones you know.

Write down what she said before you sail. You are going to need
it in a place where there is nobody to ask:
