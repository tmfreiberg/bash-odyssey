She sits them down on chairs with silver on them. Cheese,
barley, pale honey stirred into wine. It is good. They are five
days out from a harbour full of drowned men. They drink it.

There is something in it. Not poison. Something that takes the
part of a man that remembers where he is from, and puts it away
somewhere he cannot reach.

Then she stands up and touches each of them with a rod.

What is on the floor of that hall a moment later has a pig's
head, a pig's bristles, a pig's voice, and no hands.

Their minds are not touched. That is the thing. Every one of
them knows exactly what has happened and exactly who he is. None
of them can say so. None of them can say anything. They stand in
a silver-fitted hall making a noise like an animal in a market.

She drives them out to a pen behind the house and throws down
acorns for them, which is what pigs eat.

There is no pen there yet. Make one:

    less scrolls/mkdir.txt

Then put them in it. All of them, in one command. They went in
together, it happened to all of them at once, and the wildcard
you learned on the floating island is how you say "everything in
this house that is a man":

    mv circe/house/*.txt circe/house/sty

The hearth in that house is not a man and will not move. The
pattern you just used does not match hidden things. That is why
she keeps a fire nobody tends.

Go and look at them in the pen. Read one of the files. It has
not changed by a single word: the same man, the same detail you
learned at Ismarus, the same everything, in a pen, eating acorns.

Eurylochus watches the whole thing from the treeline and then
runs.
