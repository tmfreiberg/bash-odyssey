You split them.

Two parties. You take one, Eurylochus takes the other. You shake
lots in a bronze helmet to decide which of you walks into the
oak wood, and his comes out.

He does not say anything about it. He looks at you for a moment
longer than he needs to. That is all. He has been married to
your sister for twenty years and you know exactly what the look
is.

Choose five men to go with him.

Not Eurylochus -- he is going whether he likes it or not, that
is what the lot meant. Five others, off the roster, chosen by
you. There is no rule about how you choose. There is no way to
do it well.

    ls ship/crew

Move the five into the house in the wood. They walk up the track
this afternoon. They are still yours, still on your roster in
every way that matters, and the file is the man:

    mv ship/crew/somebody.txt circe/house

Five of those, with five real names in them.

Then the rest of you sit on the beach and wait, which is the
part nobody writes poems about.
