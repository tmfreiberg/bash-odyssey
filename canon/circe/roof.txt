There is a boy on your crew called Elpenor. The youngest, and
not the cleverest, and cheerful about things that do not warrant
it.

Look and see whether he is still on your roster:

    ls ship/crew


IF ELPENOR IS THERE --

Then on the last night he goes up onto the roof of that house to
sleep, because it is cooler up there and because he is very
drunk. In the morning he hears everybody moving below him. He
gets up too fast in the dark and forgets entirely where he is.

He does not come down the ladder. He comes off the edge.

His neck breaks and that is all of it. He is the first man you
have lost who was not taken by anything: no giant, no rock, no
spear, nothing that could be told as a story. He fell off a roof
because he was young, drunk, and got up too quickly.

Take him off the roster:

    rm ship/crew/elpenor.txt

Nobody notices until the ship is loaded. You are counting heads
on the beach, the count is wrong by one, and there is a period
of about ten minutes in which everybody is looking for him.
Then somebody looks up.

You do not bury him. There is no time, she has told you where
you are going, and you leave his body in that clearing lying out
in the open. Every man on that ship knows you did.

This becomes a problem. Not a metaphorical one.


IF ELPENOR IS NOT THERE --

Then he is in a grove on another island with a flower in his
hand, perfectly happy, and has been for a year and a half. He
does not fall off anything. There is nobody to leave unburied.

You will find out later what that was worth. It is not nothing.


Either way, the ship is loaded by dawn.

    less circe/departure.txt
