You are not alone in here, and the thing that is with you is not
the giant.

She has been in the room since you walked in. She was on the
beach at Ismarus and in the grove, and you have not once looked
for her.

She will not save you. That is not the arrangement. She is here
because she likes watching you work, and because she is the only
one of them who thinks you are worth the trouble.

What she wants you to notice is the olive wood drying by the
pens, and the fact that a jar came down from Ismarus for a
reason, and the fact that the first thing he will ask you is
your name.

She thinks you will know what to do about all three. She has
been wrong about you before, but not often.

She does not stay. Gods do not.
