Racks of cheese, drying, more than one household could eat in a
year. Crates of it. It would keep at sea.

Your men are looking at you and then at the cheese and then at
the door, which is still open, and they are not saying anything
because they have said it once already.

Take it and go. That was the whole plan and it was a good plan.

You are going to want to remember, later, that the cheese was
right here and the door was open and nobody made you stay.
