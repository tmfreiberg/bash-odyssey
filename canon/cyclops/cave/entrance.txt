The cave is empty and the owner is out with the flock.

Inside: cheeses in racks, lambs and kids in pens sorted by age,
pails of whey, everything in order. Whoever lives here is not a
savage. He is just enormous.

Your men want to take what they can carry and run for the ship.
That is the sensible thing and you can see it clearly.

You want to wait and see what he gives you. Guest-gifts. The
rule.

You wait.

He comes back at dusk with the flock, and he closes the door
behind him. The door is a rock. Twenty-two wagons could not move
it and neither can you, and it will not open again until he
opens it.

Look around properly before he notices you. Everything in this
room is going to matter, including what is not obvious:

    less scrolls/ls_hidden.txt

He notices you at the fire, and when he does, the night starts:

    less cyclops/cave/polyphemus.txt
