A staff of green olive wood, the size of a ship's mast, drying
by the pens. He will make a walking stick of it eventually.

You cut off a fathom of it and set your men to sharpening it,
and then you harden the point in the fire.

It is now a weapon that four men can lift, aimed at a target the
size of a dinner plate, and there is exactly one of those in
this cave.

The problem is that he is awake, and stronger than you, and if
he sees it coming you all die in this room.

He would need to be very deeply asleep. Deeper than a man sleeps
on his own. If there is wine in your hold, tonight is what the
priest meant it for.

Either way it happens at the fire, and it happens tonight:
