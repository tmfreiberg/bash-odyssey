Lambs and kids, penned by age -- the eldest, the middles, the
newborns -- each in their own pen, each pen swept.

Whoever does this is not an animal. He counts. He knows how many
he has and he will know if any are missing.

The pens are worth noticing for another reason. In the morning
the flock goes out, and it goes out past him, and he will have
his hands on every back that passes.

Under the pens, drying against the wall, there is olive wood.
