Sheep and goats, more than you bothered to count and fewer than
he has counted.

They are not afraid of you. Nothing on this side of the water is
afraid of anything, which tells you how long it has been since
anyone came here and left.

They are big. That is the thing to notice, and you will not
notice it tonight, and it will matter in the morning: they are
bred huge, like everything he keeps, and a man could get under
one of them and not be seen.

The big ram sleeps apart from the rest, nearest the door.
