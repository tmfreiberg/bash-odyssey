Four hundred yards of clear water and every man alive who was
alive an hour ago, minus the ones who are not.

Behind you on the beach, a blind giant who does not know what
happened to him. He thinks a thing called Nobody did it. He
shouted that up the hill last night and his neighbours went back
to bed.

This is the best work you have ever done. There is no one to
tell.

That is the part your men will not understand later, when they
are asking you why. It is not pride, exactly. It is that a thing
which happened to Nobody did not happen, and you have just spent
a night being nobody, and there is a limit to how long a man can
stand that.

Your name is somewhere. Either on the ship where you left it, or
in that cave under another name, depending on what you did last
night.

If you can still find it, you can shout it across the water:

    cat ship/crew/odysseus.txt

He will hear it. He will know exactly who blinded him. And he
will tell his father, who is Poseidon, who owns every mile of
water between here and Ithaca.

Nothing forces you. Nobody is watching but your men, and they
are already at the oars, and they are begging you not to.

Whatever you do, write it down. This is the one you will be
asked about:

    cat >> ship/log.txt


There is a run of empty sea after this and then an island that
should not be able to float, and does.
