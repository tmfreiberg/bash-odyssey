An island with nobody farming it, which on this coast means
either the ground is bad or something else is wrong.

Goats everywhere, unbothered, unafraid of men. You eat well for
the first time in three weeks.

Across a narrow strait, the mainland, where there is smoke. Not
a town. One fire, and sheep on the hillside above it, and a cave
mouth big enough to see from here.

There is a rule about strangers. You arrive, they feed you, you
exchange gifts, you leave as guests. It has held everywhere you
have ever been. You are curious about whether it holds here, and
you are curious in the way that has cost you before.

Take a few men and find out. Bring something from the hold --
you do not arrive at a stranger's house empty-handed, and there
is one thing down there that a priest told you to keep.
