There is dust on the road, a long way off, and it is moving.

You have seen this before, from the walls at Troy, from the
other side. It means men on the move, in a hurry, in numbers.

The Cicones inland were always more than the Cicones on the
coast. Better armed, too. They can fight from chariots or on
foot, whichever the ground wants.

You have until they arrive. You do not know how long that is.

Go down and get them off the beach. Take whatever is going to
the hold and take it now, because after this you will not be
coming back up the hill for it.
