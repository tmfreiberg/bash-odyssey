The town is called Ismarus and it belongs to the Cicones, who
fought against you at Troy. You took it at dawn. It was not
difficult.

Your ships are pulled up on the sand behind you. Your men are on
the beach with the wine jars open, roasting sheep they did not
have to pay for. Someone is singing.

You have told them twice that it is time to go. They have not
moved.

Above the beach, the town. Behind the town, hills, and a road
running inland. The Cicones who live inland will have seen the
smoke by now. They have horses. You do not.

Three places, then. The town, if you want what is left in it.
The ship, if you have had enough of this island already. And the
hills, which are the only place you can see the inland road
from, and where this day ends whether you go up there or not:

    ismarus/nightfall.txt

Read that when you are done here, and not before. Getting
anywhere means moving yourself:
