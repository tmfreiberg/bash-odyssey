The old man is called Maron. He is a priest of Apollo and he is
still alive because your men decided he should be, which is not
a thing that happens often on a morning like this.

He does not thank you. He gives you something instead, the way
you give a debt back before anyone can hold it over you.

It is wine. Twelve jars of it, and one he presses on you
personally, sealed, dark, so strong that the way to drink it is
one part wine to twenty parts water. He says nobody in his house
knows it is here except his wife and the woman who keeps the
store room.

He says: you may need this more than you need bronze.

He does not explain. Priests generally do not.

The jar is sitting here and it will stay sitting here. Nothing
is yours until it is in the hold on your ship, and putting it
there is a job for mv:

    less scrolls/mv.txt

Then get out of this town. You have been in it a long time now,
and the road inland is a road in both directions. The hills
above the town would tell you how much time you have left.
