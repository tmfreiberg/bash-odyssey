The square is quiet in the way a place is quiet an hour after
something has happened in it.

What was worth carrying has mostly been carried already. The
rest is stacked at the north end under a sailcloth: bronze,
cloth, grain. Your men will take down to the ships whatever you
tell them to take, and they will not ask twice. What you leave
in the square stays in the square.

One house is untouched. Your men went around it. When you ask
why, the man you ask looks uncomfortable and says the old man
inside is a priest of Apollo, and that there is a limit.

The sailcloth is at one end of the square and the old man's hut
is at the other. Both are worth your time and you may not have
time for both, and the hills above the town are the only place
you can see how much time that is.
