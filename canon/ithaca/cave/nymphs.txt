The cave itself. Cool, high-roofed, running back further than
the light goes.

Stone mixing bowls, stone jars, stone looms where the nymphs
weave sea purple. Bees in the roof. Water running in it all
year, from somewhere further in that nobody has followed.

Two ways in. The one on the north side is for men. The one on
the south is for gods, so no man has ever used it, so nobody on
this island has any idea where it comes out.

Your gifts are in here because a crew of foreigners carried them
up a beach at dawn while you were asleep on the sand.
