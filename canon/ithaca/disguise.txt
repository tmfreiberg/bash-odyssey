She does it with her hand. The skin on your arms goes slack,
the hair goes off your head, your eyes go dull the way an old
man's do. She dresses you in a tunic somebody has been sleeping
in, a hide with the hair worn off, a bag on a rope.

You are a beggar of about seventy. Nobody in this house will
look at you twice.

You have hidden your name once before. It was in a cave on a
hillside above a goat island, it was the best thing you ever
thought of, it worked. Then you shouted the name back across
four hundred yards of open water because a thing that happens to
Nobody does not happen, which cost you a fleet, a crew, ten
years.

Do it again. Do not undo it this time.

Your name is a file. Where it is depends on what you did that
night:

    find . -name "odysseus.txt" -o -name "outis.txt"

Move it out of sight. Not into a cave this time -- into the one
place on this island nobody has looked for twenty years:

    mkdir ithaca/beggar
    mv ship/crew/odysseus.txt ithaca/beggar/.nobody

A file whose name begins with a dot does not show in a plain
listing. You learned that in the dark with a rock across the
door. It is the same rule in your own house.

Check that you have vanished:

    ls ithaca/beggar
    ls -a ithaca/beggar

If your name is still in a cave under outis.txt, bring it home
first. It has been in the wrong country long enough.

Athena tells you where to go. Not the house: a hut, up the
island, where the swineherd lives.

    less ithaca/hut/eumaeus.txt
