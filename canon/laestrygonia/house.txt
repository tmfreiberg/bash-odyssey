The wife is inside and she is the size of a hill, and the sight
of her stops your men in the doorway.

She is pleased. She goes to the window and calls her husband in
from the assembly, and she is not calling for help, she is
calling him to dinner.

Antiphates comes. He does not ask a single question. He does not
ask where they are from or who their father is or what they
want, and there is a word for a man who does not ask, and the
word is not one you would use out loud in a house this size.

He takes the nearest one and that is the end of him.

One man off the roster. Choose him from ship/crew and rm him.
He walked up a hill because you told him to and he met a girl
at a well and she was kind to him.

The other two run. They are down the track and shouting before
Antiphates is through the door behind them, and he is not
chasing them, because he does not need to.

He is shouting.

And all the way along those cliffs, out of every house in that
country, they are coming to the edge and looking down into the
harbour at eleven ships tied up in still water with no room to
turn and no wind to turn in.
