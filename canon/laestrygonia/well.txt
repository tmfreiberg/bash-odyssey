There is a cart track up from the harbour and no fields on
either side of it, which your three men notice and mention to
each other and do not think about, because they are looking for
a town and the track is going somewhere.

They meet a girl at a well.

She is drawing water and she is perfectly friendly. She is
enormous, but so is everyone here, and by now your men have seen
enormous and it did not turn out to be the important thing about
the Cyclops.

They ask her who is king in this country.

She tells them. Her father is Antiphates, and that is his house
up there, and she will show them, and she does.

She walks them up the road talking the whole way. She is not
lying to them. She has not been told to bring anyone anywhere.
She is a girl who was asked a question and answered it, and
took three strangers home the way you would take three
strangers home.

There is no trap on this island. There is only a door, and
someone helpful holding it open.
