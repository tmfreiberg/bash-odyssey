They are sitting apart from each other, which is the first thing
you notice. Three men who have shared a bench for ten years, and
they have drifted off into the trees like men at the end of a
long party.


POLITES is against a tree with his legs out in front of him, and
when he sees you he smiles like a man seeing a friend arrive.

You ask him if he is ready to go.

He says: go where?

He is not being difficult. He has tried to remember and there is
nothing there to remember with. Ithaca is a word he knows he
used to like the sound of.


ELPENOR is flat on his back in the grass looking straight up at
the sun, which is not good for him.

He knows exactly who you are and he is delighted about it. He
wants to tell you about the flowers. He has a great deal to say
about the flowers.

You ask about Ithaca and he says yes, Ithaca, and then waits,
pleasantly, for you to say something else. The word came back to
him and brought nothing with it.

Somebody has always told him where to stand. It has worked out
for him so far.


PERIMEDES is the one who noticed.

He ate what he was given, the way you eat what you are given in
a stranger's house, and then he sat down and felt it happening,
and he is still sitting there.

He tells you so himself, quite clearly. He says he can remember
that Ithaca mattered to him this morning. He says he cannot make
it matter now, and that he has tried, and that trying is like
pushing at a door that is already open.

Then he says: if you leave me here I will be happy. You should
know that before you decide.

He is not asking to be taken and he is not asking to be left. He
is telling you the truth and letting you carry it, which is the
last useful thing he does on this island.


None of the three will walk down to the beach. It is not that
they refuse. It is that it does not occur to them, and it will
not occur to them, and there is no argument you can make to a
man who has stopped wanting things.

If they are going home it is because you carry them. Their names
went up this path this morning and they are in this grove now,
and the ship is where they belong:
