The grove is the whole country. There is nothing else here --
no fields, no boats, no walls, nothing anybody built. Just trees
and the people under them.

They are gentle and slightly slow and they are pleased to see
you, in the way that someone half asleep is pleased. They offer
you food before you have said anything, because that is what
they have and what they do. There is no trick in it. They are
being kind.

It is a flower. They eat it and they eat nothing else.

Your three men are sitting among them. They are not hurt, not
held, not drugged asleep in a corner. They are fine, and that is
the difficulty. They are perfectly fine.

Go and talk to them. They are at the far side, under the trees:
