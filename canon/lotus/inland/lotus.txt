They are holding one out to you.

It is not a drug, exactly. Nobody here is confused. They know
where they are and they are glad about it. What they have lost
is the sense that anywhere else has a claim on them.

Nine days of wind. Ten years of war before that. A wife you
would not recognise in the street and a son who was a baby, and
a kingdom that has probably given you up, and between here and
there, if the last ten years are anything to go by, a great deal
more sea.

Or this. Which is warm, and here, and quite easy.

If you are staying, then the log is finished and it only needs
its last line. One line, replacing all of it, because none of
the rest was ever going anywhere:

    cat > ship/log.txt

If you are not staying, go and get your men, and do not eat
anything on the way down.
