The path is worn smooth and nobody is watching it. No gate, no
dog, nobody posted where the track comes up from the beach.

Your three went up here this morning and they have not come back
and they have not sent word.

There is no sound of fighting. There is no sound of anything at
all, which is worse, because there are people at the end of this
path and people make noise.

You go up yourself. It is not far.
