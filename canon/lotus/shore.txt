Nine days of wind off Cape Malea, blowing the wrong way, and on
the tenth you make land somewhere nobody can name.

There is fresh water here. That is the only reason to stop and
it is reason enough. The men fill the jars and eat on the sand.

Inland there is a path, and people living at the end of it who
have not come down to see who you are. That is unusual. Nobody
comes down to look at twelve ships unless there is something
wrong with them or something wrong with you.

Write the crossing down before you forget it -- nine days is a
long time to lose -- and then send three men up to find out what
kind of people live here.
