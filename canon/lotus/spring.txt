The jars are full. Your men are aboard, however many of them
you have.

If your log is intact, read it. Nine days and a town in Thrace,
in your own handwriting, and it is worth something to have it.

If your log is one line long, that is what you kept, and there
is no getting the rest back. Read it anyway. It will be a while
before you understand what it cost you.

is there, you still have your voyage. If grep finds nothing, you
know what happened.

Then get off this island before anybody else eats anything.

writing about, and then an island with goats on it and no people,
