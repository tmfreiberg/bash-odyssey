She comes down to the rock herself.

She says: unhappy man, stop sitting here. I am letting you go.

You do not believe her for a moment. Seven years of her offering
you eternity, then this, on an ordinary morning, with no reason
given -- you have been on the receiving end of gods for a decade
now.

So you do the one thing you have learned to do.

You tell her: goddess, there is no raft on this island that
would cross that gulf. I will not set foot on any vessel until
you swear the great oath that you are not planning some other
harm for me.

She laughs. She strokes your face. She says: you are a
scoundrel; you never think a straight thought; what a thing to
have worked out.

Then she swears it. Earth, sky, the water of Styx, the whole
formula, the oath that binds the blessed gods themselves.

Write it down. You have done this once before, in an oak wood,
with a different goddess, for the same reason:

Put it in ogygia/oath.txt, in her words or in your own.

Afterwards she feeds you. She sits opposite you eating nectar
while you eat human food, which is the last domestic thing that
happens on this island.

Then she asks you one more time, without much force, whether you
would rather stay.

You say no. She says: go on, then. Take the tools.
