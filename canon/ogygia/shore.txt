Nine days on a keel lashed to a mast. On the tenth night
something puts you on a beach.

You do not remember arriving. You remember water, then sand
under your face, then a very long time of neither.

Ogygia. The navel of the sea. Not on the way to anywhere, not
near anything, visited by nobody, because there is nothing here
to visit except her.

She finds you above the tideline. She is a goddess, daughter of
Atlas, who holds the pillars that keep the sky off the earth.
Her name is Calypso, which in your language is close to the word
for hiding something.

She takes you in. She feeds you. She asks nothing at all.

You are the only man on this island. You will be the only man on
this island for a very long time.
