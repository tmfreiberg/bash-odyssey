cat -- print a file

Dumps a whole file onto the screen at once and gives you the
prompt back:

    cat shore.txt

For anything long, less is better -- it lets you scroll and
search. cat is for short files and for a second thing, which is
more interesting.

Give cat no filename at all and it will read what you type
instead:

    cat

Nothing happens. It is waiting for you. Type a line, press
Enter, type another. When you are done, press Ctrl-D on a line
of its own, and cat prints back everything you typed.

That is useless on its own. It stops being useless in the next
scroll.

cat is short for concatenate, which is what it does with more
than one file:

    cat first.txt second.txt

Both, in order, as one stream.
