cd -- change directory

Moves you. Name a place that ls has shown you:

    cd town

To go back the way you came, use two dots. They always mean
"the directory above this one":

    cd ..

You can chain them with slashes. This goes up one level and
then into the hills:

    cd ../hills

And on its own, with nothing after it, cd takes you home:

    cd

If you are ever lost, pwd will tell you where you ended up.

One more thing, and it will save you trouble for the rest of
this voyage.

When the story tells you to read a scroll, it writes the path
from the top of the game -- from play, where you started. If you
have walked somewhere since then, that path will not work from
where you are standing.

The fix is not to walk back. It is to reach up:

    less ../../scrolls/mv.txt

Count the directories between you and the top, and use two dots
for each. If you lose track, pwd will tell you where you are.

Note that cd on its own does not help here. That takes you to
your home directory on this machine, which is somewhere else
entirely. To get back to the top of the game, walk up with two
dots until ls shows you the islands again.
