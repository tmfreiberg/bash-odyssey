grep -- find lines that match

Give it something to look for and a file to look in:

    grep Ismarus ship/log.txt

Every line containing that word, printed. Nothing else.

Case matters. Ismarus and ismarus are different words to grep
unless you tell it otherwise:

    grep -i ismarus ship/log.txt

To find the lines that do not match, use -v. To count them
instead of printing them, use -c:

    grep -c wine ship/log.txt

If grep prints nothing at all, it found nothing at all. On a log
you wrote yourself, that means something.
