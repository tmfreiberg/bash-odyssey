ls -a -- list everything, including hidden things

A file whose name begins with a dot is hidden. Not locked, not
encrypted. Just not shown, unless you ask:

    ls -a

Every directory you have ever been in has had things in it you
were not shown. Your own home directory is full of them.

There is nothing clever about it. The rule is one character
long: a name starting with . does not appear in a plain ls.

    ls -a

Do it here. This is a cave with one door and a rock across it,
and the difference between a man who checks and a man who does
not is the whole of the rest of your life.
