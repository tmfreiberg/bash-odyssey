mv -- move

Takes something from one place and puts it in another. The thing
first, the destination second:

    mv maron_wine.jar hold

The jar is now in the hold. It is not in the hut any more. That
is the difference between mv and cp: cp leaves the original
where it was, mv does not.

mv also renames. If the second name is not a directory, mv
treats it as the new name for the thing:

    mv maron_wine.jar ballast.txt

That is one file with a new name, not two files. The old name is
gone.

Nothing warns you if the new name is already in use. mv will
write over what is there and say nothing about it. If you are
not certain, -i makes it ask first:

    mv -i maron_wine.jar hold

You can move something without standing next to it, by giving
the full route to it:

    mv town/hut/maron_wine.jar ship/hold
