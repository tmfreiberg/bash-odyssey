tee -- send output two ways at once

A pipe passes what one command prints into another. Redirection
sends it into a file. Both have the same limitation: whatever
you do with the output, you stop seeing it.

    cat log.txt > copy.txt

That works. Nothing appears on your screen.

tee does both:

    cat log.txt | tee copy.txt

The contents go into copy.txt. The contents also print, so you
watch the thing you are saving as it goes past.

The name comes from plumbing. A T-shaped joint takes one flow,
sends it two directions.

By default tee overwrites the file, in the way > does. To add to
the end instead, the way >> does:

    cat log.txt | tee -a copy.txt

It takes more than one destination:

    cat log.txt | tee copy_one.txt copy_two.txt

It is most useful in the middle of a longer pipeline, where you
want to keep an intermediate result without stopping the flow:

    cat log.txt | grep Ithaca | tee found.txt | wc -l

That prints a count. It also leaves you a file of what was
counted, which is the part you would otherwise have thrown away.
