You are through in under a minute. That is the whole of it.

The water goes flat on the far side the way water does. The men
who are still rowing keep rowing. Nobody says anything for a
long time.

Then somebody at the back begins to weep, which starts the rest
of them.

You take the armour off. You do it yourself, sitting down,
badly, because your hands are not steady. Nobody helps you. It
occurs to you while you are doing it that every man aboard now
knows you knew.

Count what is left:

    find ship/crew -type f | wc -l

Write it down. All of it, including what you did not tell them
before you went in:

    cat >> ship/log.txt

Ahead, low on the water, there is an island with cattle on it.

You can hear them from a long way out: lowing in the meadows,
sheep bleating behind them, the sound of a large, well-kept,
entirely peaceful farm.

You have been warned about this place twice. Once by a dead
prophet on a black beach at the edge of the world. Once by
Circe, in her own house, in words she chose carefully.
