You can hear it before you can see it.

Smoke standing off the water where there is nothing burning.
Surf on rock. Under both, at intervals, a noise like something
very large swallowing.

Two cliffs. The strait between them is a bowshot across. There
is no third route, no way round the outside, no waiting for
better weather, because the weather has nothing to do with it.

Circe named this place the same night she named the Sirens. She
went through it twice. She made you say it back to her.

The left-hand cliff has a cave in it, high up, out of reach of
anything you could throw. The right-hand side is lower, with a
fig tree on it, under which the water does something you will
not believe until you see it.

She said: one of these takes some of you. The other takes all of
you. There is no third thing on offer here.

Before you go in, count what you have. This is the last hour in
which the number is what it is:

    less scylla/stock.txt

This strait ends on the far side, in the calm:

    scylla/after.txt

Read that when you are through it, not before.
