Ten years. Twelve ships out of Troy, then eleven, then one.

You have never once sat down with the whole of it in front of
you. There has not been an hour when it was possible. There is
one now, on flat water, half a mile short of the narrows.

    less scrolls/find.txt

Start with the ship. Every file aboard her, wherever it sits:

    find ship -type f

Then the men, counted rather than read:

    find ship/crew -type f | wc -l

Then the things you have left scattered across this sea.

A jar of wine. An old priest of Apollo pressed it on you in a
sacked town in Thrace, in the first week, before any of this.
He said you would need it more than bronze. He did not say what
for:

    find . -name "maron_wine.jar"

Four answers to that. It is in a hut in Ismarus, because you
never carried it down. It is in your hold, because you carried
it down but never opened it. It is in a cave on a hillside above
a goat island, because you gave it to something that had never
tasted wine. Or there is no answer at all, because you poured
the last of it out that night, then got rid of the jar.

Only one of those four cost you nothing.

Your own name. You gave it away in a cave on a hillside above a
goat island. Whether it is still in that cave under another word
depends on what you did that night:

    find . -name "outis.txt"
    find . -name "odysseus.txt"

The winds. A king on a floating island handed you every one of
them tied up in an oxhide bag. Some are on his beach. Some are
in the bag. Some got out:

    find . -name "*.wind"

The men you left in a grove with flowers in their hands, if you
left any:

    find lotus -type f

The plant a god pulled out of the ground for you on a path in an
oak wood:

    find . -name ".moly"

Her oath, if you wrote it down:

    find . -name "oath.txt"

What you poured for the dead:

    find . -name "trench_offering.txt"

That is the voyage. Not the story of it -- the residue. Every
one of those results is a thing you decided, or failed to
decide, on an afternoon when it did not feel like a decision.

No other man's list looks like yours.

    less scylla/cliff.txt
