Odysseus, son of Laertes, of Ithaca.

Sacker of cities. The man who thought of the horse.

Twenty years of work went into that sentence and it is worth
something in every port on this sea, which is the only wage he
has ever cared about. He says it on arrival, unprompted, to
anyone. He has never once considered not saying it.

He is on the beach with the others, waiting for himself to
decide something.
