IF PERIMEDES IS ON THAT ROSTER --

Then it is Perimedes handling the rope, with Eurylochus on the
other side pulling it through.

Perimedes is the steady one. The man you would pick to hold a
rope you needed held. That is not decoration: on a flowered
island a long time ago he sat under a tree telling you, quite
clearly, that he could remember Ithaca mattering to him that
morning, that he could not make it matter any more, that trying
was like pushing at a door already open.

You carried him down to the beach.

He is the only man on this ship who has been on the other side
of wanting the wrong thing. He knows precisely what it looks
like from inside. When you start shouting he is not going to be
confused about it for one second.


IF PERIMEDES IS SITTING IN A GROVE ON ANOTHER ISLAND --

Then it is Eurylochus with two of the ordinary men, doing a
competent job of a thing none of them has done before.

They will hold. Probably. They are your crew, they were given an
instruction, they cannot hear you countermand it.

But nobody on this deck has ever wanted something the way you
are about to want it. They are going to be working from a rule
rather than from understanding, which is thinner rope than you
would like.


The ship comes within hearing.
