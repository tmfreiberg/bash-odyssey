Circe met you on the beach when you came back from the dead. She
had bread, meat, red wine, no questions. She knew where you had
been.

She kept you one more night. She spent it telling you what is
between here and Ithaca, in order, with the names.

The first of them is two women in a meadow on an island, who
sing.

She was precise about this one, more than about any of the
others. Any man who hears them stops. He does not decide to
stop. There is no moment where he weighs it. He simply never
goes home, his wife never learns what became of him, his bones
end up in a heap in a field of flowers.

The instruction is: seal every man's ears with wax before you
come within hearing.

Then she said the other part, which she did not have to say, in
the tone of someone who knows exactly who she is talking to.

If you want to hear it, you can. Have them tie you upright to
the mast, hand and foot, tight. Tell them before you start that
you are going to beg. Tell them that when you beg they are to
tie you tighter rather than looser.

You have not slept much since.
