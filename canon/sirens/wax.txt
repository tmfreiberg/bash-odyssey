The wind drops.

Not gradually. One moment there is a following breeze, the next
the sail is slack, the water is flat as a floor, the whole sea
has gone quiet in a way that is not weather. Some god flattened
it so that nothing would interfere with the hearing.

The men stow the sail. They get on the oars.

You cut up a great round of beeswax with your sword, work it
soft in your hands until it gives, then go down the benches one
man at a time putting it into their ears.

Every man aboard. Not one exception among them.

They cannot hear you from the moment you finish. From here to
the far side of that island, whatever happens, whatever you do,
they are working from what they were told before you started.
