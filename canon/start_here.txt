NOSTOS

A homecoming, in thirteen episodes and twenty-six commands.

Troy has fallen. You are Odysseus, and you are going home to
Ithaca. It should take a few weeks.

It is going to take ten years.

The story will tell you where things are, what is happening, and
what you need to do next. Working out how to do it is the game.
Nobody is going to hand you the exact words -- but nobody is
going to leave you standing in the dark either, and any time you
need a tool you have not used before, you will be told where to
read about it. They are in a directory called scrolls, and you
read one the same way you are reading this:

    less scrolls/pwd.txt
    less scrolls/ls.txt

Those paths are written from here, the top of the game. Once you
start moving around they will not work as written, and the scroll
on cd explains how to reach back up. Some people keep a second
window open, parked here, just for reading scrolls. That is
allowed. Everything about the way you sit at this machine is
allowed.

There is a directory here for every island this voyage touches.
Each one tells you, when you land on it, which file that island
ends at. Wander as much as you like in between; when you have
read the ending file, that island is done with you.

Read those two scrolls now. The first tells you where you are
standing, the second how to look around. Then go to the first
island, which is ismarus, where you have already done the damage.

    less ismarus/shore.txt
