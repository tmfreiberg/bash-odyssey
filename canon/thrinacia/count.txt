Seven herds in seven meadows, running down to the water.

    ls thrinacia/herd

Three hundred fifty animals. Count them rather than taking the
number on trust:

    ls thrinacia/herd | wc -l

Read one. They are all like this:

    less thrinacia/herd/cow_001.txt

Nothing on this island has aged. The two herdswomen have nothing
to do but look, because there are no calves to bring on, no old
beasts to cull, no losses to make up. The count was three
hundred fifty before Troy. It will be three hundred fifty after
Ithaca.

Which means any change in that number is visible from a very
long way off. Including from the sky.


Now do the one thing you have never bothered to do on this
voyage.

There is a scroll you were given at Ismarus, in the first week,
before any of this. It is about copying. You have never once
needed it. You have deleted men in four places since then with
no way back, because a way back was never on offer.

    less scrolls/cp.txt

Copy the herd. Not the animals -- there is no copying those.
The record of them: every file in that meadow, duplicated
somewhere it will survive you falling asleep.

    cp -R thrinacia/herd thrinacia/herd_as_found

That is the -R from the scroll, which copies a directory with
everything inside it. Three hundred fifty files, one command,
no time at all.

Nobody is making you do it. It is the difference between knowing
tomorrow morning exactly what was taken from you, or saying
"some" about it for the rest of your life.

    less thrinacia/locked.txt
