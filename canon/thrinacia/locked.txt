Look at the cattle the way a filesystem looks at them:

    ls -l thrinacia/herd

Every one of those files says the same thing in its first
column. Readable. Not writable. Not by you, not by your crew,
not by anybody who has rowed in here off the sea.

    less scrolls/chmod.txt

That is what "not yours" means in this place. You may look at
them. You may walk among them. You may count them as often as
you like. You may not alter them by one hair.

Set it yourself, on the whole herd at once, so that nobody
aboard can claim afterwards that the rule was unclear:

    chmod -w thrinacia/herd/*.txt

Read the notice back:

    ls -l thrinacia/herd | head

A permission is not a wall. Read the scroll if you have not: the
owner of a file can always take the notice down. It does not
stop a determined man. It records that he had to be determined.

There is a paragraph at the end of that scroll for anyone
playing on Windows. The short of it: taking away write
permission behaves the same everywhere, which is the half this
island uses.

Nothing here turns on whether the notice held anyway.

It turns on what is standing in that field when you wake up.

    less thrinacia/wind.txt
