The blood goes into the trench. They come up out of the dark.

Not a few. Brides, unmarried boys, old men who worked their
whole lives, girls new to grief, men in armour with the wounds
still on them. They come around that trench in a mass, from
every direction, making a noise that is not any noise you have
heard.

Your sword is out. Circe was exact about this: not one of them
drinks before Tiresias drinks. Not one.

You hold them off with a blade while they push.
