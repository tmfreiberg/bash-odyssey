You would have stayed longer.

You wanted the older ones, the famous dead, the men from before
Troy. You were standing there in the dark on the shore of the
world working out who else to call up, which tells you something
about yourself that you will think about later.

Then the crowd grows.

Thousands of them, from every direction, all at once, making
that noise. It rises. Somewhere behind them, out in the dark,
something is coming that they are getting out of the way of --
one of the old things, with a head that turns people to stone,
sent up by the queen of this place to see who has been digging
holes on her beach.

Terror takes you. Straightforward, uncomplicated terror, the
kind you have not felt since you were a boy.

You run.

Back down the shore, into the boat, both men already at the
oars, nobody needing to be told. You get the ship out into the
river of Ocean on oars alone. Then the current takes her.

Then a wind comes up behind you, from nowhere, without being
asked for.

Write it down before it fades. All of it: the trench, the
prophet, the cattle you have been warned about, your mother
three times in your arms with nothing in them:

    cat >> ship/log.txt

Read what is in your trench, too, one last time. Four pourings
or one, whichever you managed, that is what you brought to the
dead:

    cat underworld/trench_offering.txt

You are going back to Circe's island first. You promised
somebody, or you did not, depending on what happened on a roof.

After that there is a stretch of flat water where the wind
drops, where the sea goes quiet in a way that is not natural,
where you will hear something over it.

    less sirens/shore.txt
