The north wind takes you all day. She arranged it. By dark you
are somewhere the sea stops behaving like the sea.

There is a shore here with no sun on it. Not night: there is no
night, because there was never any day. Cloud sits on the water.
Fog stands in the willows. A grove of black poplar runs along
the bank above the tideline, every tree of it barren.

People live here. Homer calls them the Cimmerians. Nobody has
ever seen them, nobody has ever spoken to one, nobody knows what
they eat. They are simply the people who live where the light
does not come, so that somebody does.

Your men will not get out of the boat.

You go up the beach alone with two of them behind you carrying a
ram, a black ewe, a jar, a sack of barley. Neither man speaks
for the whole walk. One of them is crying quietly, which he does
not know you can hear.

Circe gave you the rite. It is in four parts. The order of them
is the entire thing:

    less underworld/trench.txt

This island ends when you run, which you will:

    underworld/flight.txt

Read that when there is nothing left down here you can stand,
not before.
