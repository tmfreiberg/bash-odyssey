Dig it a forearm long, a forearm wide, at the place where the
two rivers run into the third.

The trench is a file. It is empty because you have not dug it
yet, which is the same thing here.

Then pour. Three libations, in this order, for all the dead
together:

    first, milk mixed with honey
    then sweet wine
    then water

Over the top of the three, scatter white barley.

Every one of those goes on top of what is already in the trench.
That is what a libation is: a thing added to a thing. If any
part of it wipes out the part before it, you have poured one
offering into a hole in the ground four times, the dead will
smell nothing, you will stand on this beach until the sun that
is not there goes down.

You know the two characters that separate those outcomes. You
have known them since Ismarus. One of them is the character that
empties a file. There was an island covered in flowers where
